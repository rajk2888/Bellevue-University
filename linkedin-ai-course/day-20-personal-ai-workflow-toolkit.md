Day 20/30 — Building your personal AI workflow toolkit

---

Twenty days in, we've covered the "how it works" and the "how to prompt
it." Today's post is about turning that into a system you actually use
every day, instead of ad hoc chats you forget about.

A few habits that compound over time:

→ A prompt library — save your best prompts (Day 15) somewhere searchable,
organized by task type: writing, analysis, coding, brainstorming.

→ A default "context pack" — for recurring tasks, keep a short doc with
the background the model always needs (your audience, your voice, your
constraints) and paste it in rather than re-explaining every time.

→ Task-fit awareness — know which tasks AI genuinely accelerates
(first drafts, summarizing, brainstorming, boilerplate code) versus which
ones it just feels fast on but need heavy verification (facts, final
copy, anything with real consequences).

→ A verification habit — pair every AI-assisted deliverable with the
specific check from Day 19 that matches its stakes.

None of this requires new tools. It's the same discipline you'd apply to
any recurring process — templates, checklists, and knowing your tools'
limits. The people who look like "AI power users" usually aren't running
fancier prompts; they've just built a system around ordinary ones.
