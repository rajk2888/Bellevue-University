Day 30/30 — Putting it all together: your AI learning roadmap

---

Thirty days ago, "AI" was a vague, slightly intimidating buzzword. If
you've followed along, you now have a real mental model of how it works
end to end:

→ Phase 1 — the fundamentals: what AI/ML/deep learning/GenAI actually mean,
how machines learn from data, how neural networks and LLMs work, why
hallucinations happen, and the ethical stakes underneath it all.

→ Phase 2 — using it well: how to write prompts that actually get good
results, chain-of-thought, role prompting, RAG, agents, multimodal
models, and how to evaluate whether AI output is genuinely good.

→ Phase 3 — how it gets built and shipped: data pipelines, feature
engineering, evaluation metrics, overfitting, fine-tuning vs prompting,
deployment, monitoring, and the real economics of running AI in
production.

None of this required a PhD. It required breaking a scary-sounding field
into small, honest, jargon-free pieces — the same way you'd learn anything
else.

If you want to go deeper from here, pick one thread that pulled at you the
most — prompting, ML engineering, or the ethics side — and go build
something small with it this week. Understanding compounds fastest through
use, not more reading.

Thank you for following this series. Which day's concept are you going to
actually put into practice first?
