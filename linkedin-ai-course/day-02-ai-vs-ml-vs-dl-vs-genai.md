Day 2/30 — AI, ML, Deep Learning, GenAI: the family tree

---

I used to use these four terms interchangeably. That was a mistake — they're
nested inside each other, like Russian dolls.

→ Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broadest term: any system that mimics
intelligent behavior.

→ Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI: systems that improve at a task by
learning from data, rather than being explicitly programmed.

→ Deep Learning is a subset of ML: it uses layered neural networks
(more on those in a few days) to learn especially complex patterns — the
technique behind image recognition and speech-to-text.

→ Generative AI is a subset of deep learning: models trained to produce new
content — text, images, audio — rather than just classify or predict a
number.

So when someone says "our product uses AI," that tells you almost nothing.
When they say "we fine-tuned a deep learning model for generation," now you
know exactly where it sits on the map.

I keep this hierarchy pinned in my notes because it instantly filters out
marketing fluff from real technical claims.
