Day 18/30 — Multimodal AI: text, image, audio, and video together

---

For years, AI models were specialists: one model for text, a different one
for images, another for speech. Multimodal AI collapses that into a single
model that can take in — and often generate — more than one type of data
at once.

Under the hood, the trick is surprisingly elegant: images get chopped into
patches and audio gets chopped into short segments, and both get converted
into the same kind of embedding vectors (Day 8) that text tokens use. Once
everything is just numbers in the same "meaning space," a single model can
reason across all of them together — describing an image, answering a
question about a chart, transcribing and summarizing audio, or generating
an image from a text description.

Why this matters practically: you can now hand a model a screenshot of a
bug and ask it to explain the error, upload a photo of a whiteboard and get
organized notes, or feed it a chart and ask for the trend in plain
language — no separate specialized tool needed.

The frontier right now is video and real-time voice — models that can
watch a video and understand what's happening over time, or hold a live,
natural voice conversation. Both are advancing fast.
